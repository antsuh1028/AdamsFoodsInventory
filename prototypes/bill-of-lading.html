Things to try

  1. BOL 30300 is your photo. Tap the short plate line and scan two of its pallet tags: it reads "30 + 30".
  2. Scan the third short plate pallet into it: refused, the line wants 60.
  3. Tap the brisket line and scan its pallet tag.
  4. Pick Loaded by and Driver, then Send to NTI, then mark it received at NTI.
  5. New BOL: scan pallets of different lots one after another. Each lot gets its own line; a second pallet of a lot joins its line ("30 + 30").
  6. All but two: on BOL 30301 tap the chuck roll line (22 cases) and scan its pallet tag (24 boxes). Then tap 2 blue boxes to leave them behind, and Done.
  7. After that, See tag on the chuck roll pallet: the reprint for the 2 boxes left.
  8. Print either BOL: each pallet's box weights, ten to a row, with subtotals and the total lb.
  9. Press See tag on a pallet: what the pallet tag and its barcode would look like. Scan it with a real scanner.
